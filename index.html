Pricing & FAQ

Straight answers before you book.

Website projects typically run $4,500–$18,000 depending on size and automation. Care plans start at $149/month. You get an exact quote on your free call — no obligation.

Book Free Strategy Call
How much does a new website cost?

Most projects land between $4,500 and $18,000. A brochure site for a restaurant or contractor is on the lower end; a larger site with multiple locations, online ordering, or advanced AI workflows is higher. You will get a fixed quote after your strategy call — no hourly surprises.

How long does it take to launch?

Three to four weeks for a typical small-business website, measured from the day we receive your content and photos. Larger projects take six to eight weeks. We give you a dated schedule at the strategy step.

Do you only work with businesses in one city?

No. We’re a Central Valley studio and know the region well — Fresno, Modesto, Stockton, Sacramento, Bakersfield — but we build websites and run local SEO for businesses anywhere in California, and we work remotely with clients nationwide. Local SEO works the same in any market.

What exactly is AI appointment booking?

An assistant on your website chats with visitors in plain language, answers common questions, checks your real availability, and books the appointment or table straight into your calendar or reservation system — 24/7. You approve the rules; it never double-books.

Do you handle hosting, email, and maintenance?

Yes. Our care plans cover managed hosting, a custom business email setup, software updates, security monitoring, automatic backups, small content edits, and a monthly analytics report.

What if I already have a website?

We do redesigns constantly. We audit what is working, keep your best content and search rankings, and rebuild the rest on a faster, easier-to-manage platform — then add the AI and SEO layers.
